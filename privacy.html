Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-22

1) Data minimization (only what’s needed)

We minimize the data collected. The form is designed to collect only what’s needed to write your copy: offer, audience, placement, proof, constraints, plus an email for delivery/clarification. Do not submit sensitive personal data.

2) AI disclosure

We use AI to draft content. You are responsible for verifying factual claims, numbers, testimonials, and compliance before publishing. The form includes a required confirmation checkbox.

3) Retention & deletion

Retention rule: briefs and associated intake data are deleted after 30 days, unless a longer period is required for ongoing support, dispute handling, or by law.

4) How your data is used

Your inputs are used to generate an approved Markdown brief and produce copy deliverables based on your selected package. If you provide a webhook, the approved brief may be transmitted to your automation workflow for processing and delivery.

5) Security measures

We take reasonable steps to protect data in transit and at rest where applicable. If you automate email sending, prefer a reputable email provider and enable domain authentication: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

6) Rate limiting / spam control

This form includes anti-spam measures (honeypot + client cooldown). Optional controls include CAPTCHA (Cloudflare Turnstile) and blocking free email domains for B2B workflows. For webhook processing, add server-side rate limiting and CAPTCHA token verification.

7) Your choices

You choose delivery format (Google Doc link and/or plain text email). You can also omit optional fields (name, company, website) and still generate a brief.

8) Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: eyeopenworlds0@gmail.com
