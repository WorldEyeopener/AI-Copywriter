FAQ

Short answers to keep things moving.

Do you email the customer automatically?
A GPT can’t send email by itself. This page can POST the approved brief to your webhook (Zapier/Make/Vercel), where your automation can generate copy and send it via an email provider. If no webhook is set, it opens a prefilled email draft to the owner.
What data do you collect?
Only what’s needed to write the copy (offer, audience, placement, proof, constraints + email for delivery). Avoid sensitive data. See Privacy Policy for details.
How do you prevent spam?
Includes a honeypot field, client cooldown, optional free-email blocking, and optional CAPTCHA. For webhooks, add server-side rate limiting too.
Can I deliver via Google Docs?
Yes — customers can select Google Doc and/or plain text. If you automate, your backend can create a Doc and email the share link.
